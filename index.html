Laws in this game

How Mega Smash football works

Each side has thirteen players on the field. A set is six tackles. After the sixth tackle the ball is handed over. A play-the-ball follows every completed tackle. The dummy half is usually the hooker.

Passes must travel backwards or flat. A forward pass is a scrum to the other team. A knock-on is the same. Kicks are not knock-ons. The kicker cannot regather their own kick immediately.

Ground the ball in the in-goal, inside touch and before the dead-ball line, for a try (4). Under an active tackle you must dive or get the ball down. If you are held up, the defence drops out from their goal line.

Conversions and penalty goals are worth 2. A field goal is worth 1 and stays in play if it misses. A kick into touch on the full is a penalty. A ball that bounces into touch restarts with a twenty-metre tap. A forty-twenty gives the kicking team the tap.

Markers may stand at the ruck. Everyone else must retreat. Encroachment inside the referee’s margin is six again. The clock runs until the hooter, then the half ends at the next stoppage. Extra time is golden point.

On attack, the stick moves the ball carrier. Up the screen is always the try line you are attacking, including after half-time. Pass, kick, sprint, and dive are the attacking buttons. On defence you move a defender, tackle, and switch to the next nearest teammate.

Keyboard: W A S D or arrows to run, Shift to sprint, J or Space to pass, F to tackle, M for Mega Hit when it lights up, K to kick, L to dive, Tab to switch, Enter to tap or play the ball, C to change camera, Esc to pause.

Mega Hit and illegal smash options appear only in short windows. Sometimes the referee misses it. Dominant contacts can drop into slow-mo with blood spray. Tempers can spark a punch-up, kick, wrestle, or an all-in brawl.

Match settings

Saved on this device

Movement stays in real time. Clock speed only advances the match clock. Nothing here is paid, and the game is playable with the volume at zero. Settings, season, and career stay on this device. On Android, a banner ad can sit under the menus and is hidden during a match.

Privacy

What stays on this device

Mega Smash: Rugby League 27 does not ask you to create an account, sell your save, or send that save to a server. City names and jersey colours are used for atmosphere. This is not an official NRL product.

The Android app can show a Google AdMob banner on menus. That banner is hidden during a live match. The browser game does not load ads. The save stays on this device.

Settings, the Meridian Cup season, career totals, match history, and achievements are stored in this app’s private storage under the names meridian-league-save and meridian-league-save-backup. A career name is whatever you type, and it never leaves the device.

Clearing the app’s storage, or the browser site data, deletes that save. There is no cloud copy.
